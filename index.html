What is the difference between term life and whole life insurance?

Term life covers you for a set number of years, often 20 or 30, and then it ends. It is the lowest cost way to put a large death benefit in place. Whole life covers you for your whole life, as long as the premiums are paid, and it builds cash value over time. It costs more because it is built to never expire.

How do producers decide how much coverage a family needs?

Many producers use the DIME method. They add up debts, income for the years your family would need it, the mortgage, and school costs. Then they subtract savings and any coverage you already have. The gap that is left is the working number for the amount.
