How it works

The check follows the same path a careful producer uses with a new family.

1

Map the household

Who earns, who depends on it, what is owed, and how long the money has to last. No product talk yet.

2

Size the need

Producers add up debts, income years, the mortgage, and school costs, then subtract what you already have. That gap is the number that matters.

3

Match the kind, flag the traps

The shape of the need points to a kind of coverage: a set number of years, a lifetime, or a blend. Your answers also surface traps like policy loans and fast funding.

Want the version that fits your household? Take the Clearline Check. Ten plain questions, about two minutes, answers stay in your browser.
