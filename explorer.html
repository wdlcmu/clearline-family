The funnel, step by step

    Who's who: the roles, in one line each

    Guardian

    Raises the minor kids

    Named in your wills. The human being who brings them up if you're both gone. Courts decide this only if you don't.

    Custodian (UTMA)

    Manages a child's money until 21

    The default when insurance names a minor, then hands it all over outright at 21.

    Trustee

    Manages trust assets by the trust's rules

    ILIT trustee: owns the policies, pays premiums, distributes per the trust. Family trustee: runs the kids' inheritance, including past 21.

    Financial POA agent

    Manages YOUR money while you're alive but can't

    Pays bills, handles accounts. Authority ends at death. That's when the executor takes over.

    Healthcare proxy

    Makes medical decisions if you can't

    Works alongside your written health care directive. Also ends at death.

    Executor

    Settles the estate, then done

    Named in the will. Guides assets through probate into the trust, pays final debts and taxes, closes up shop.

    Sources checked September 2026: WA Department of Revenue estate-tax tables and FAQ (the $3M exclusion, Table W 10 to 20% rates, no portability); IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32 (the $15M federal exclusion and $19,000 annual gift exclusion). A QA pass verified every tax claim on this page against those primary sources, including the per-decedent math: Washington taxes each spouse's estate separately, so simultaneous death is tested against two $3M exclusions, not one.
